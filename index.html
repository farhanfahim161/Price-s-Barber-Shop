About Us

A Cut Above the Rest in Saint Augustine

Welcome to Price's Barber Shop, where the timeless tradition of barbering meets contemporary style. Located in the historic city of Saint Augustine, Florida, we've been serving our community with pride and precision.

Our skilled barbers bring years of experience and passion to every haircut. Whether you're looking for a classic cut, a modern fade, or a traditional straight razor shave, we deliver exceptional results with attention to detail.
